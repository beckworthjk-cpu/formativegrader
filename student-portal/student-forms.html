⏱️How balances get updated

Nothing on these forms updates instantly. When you submit either form, it goes into a queue that [TEACHER NAME] reviews and processes [UPDATE FREQUENCY] — not the moment you hit submit.

You do not need to check your balance multiple times a day. Submitting the Balance Check Form again and again doesn't make the answer come back any faster — it just adds more requests for [TEACHER NAME] to go through, which actually slows things down for everyone, including you. One check is enough. If you haven't heard back by the next update, it's still in the queue — please be patient rather than resubmitting.

✅Tips for using the forms correctly

  • Use your real name and correct student ID exactly as it appears on your roster — mismatched info is the #1 reason a request gets delayed.
  • Submit one request per form per thing you're asking about. Duplicate submissions don't get processed faster; they just create confusion about which one is "the real one."
  • Double-check your answers before you hit submit — you won't get a confirmation email back right away.
  • Redemption requests are reviewed before they're fulfilled, so submitting the form is a request, not an instant purchase.
  • If something looks wrong (a redemption didn't go through, your balance seems off), don't just resubmit the form — email [TEACHER NAME] directly at [TEACHER EMAIL] so it can be looked into.
